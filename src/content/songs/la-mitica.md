# Song Q&A

## What's the song about?

"La mítica" means "the legendary one." The lyrics are a tough, sexy braggadocio swagger — she's announcing herself. Pure chemistry, epic life, and she wants to be the one running you. It's got that confidence that doesn't ask permission.

## How this one started

At the very beginning was a very Latin sounding beat. I had just been playing around but I really liked how much the rhythm and beat just stood up. It was almost like a march or something.

I then started playing around with an arrangement, and got it to something I liked a lot, then looked on Splice for some lyrics that sounded good with it. And I was so pleased to find this particular artist, who had just the right sound for the song. It really cemented everything else and made it all come together.

## Working with vocals in a language you don't speak

It did change things a little. I'm embarrassed to say, at first I didn't even know what the lyrics meant. I had to look them up, and when I did, it did alter where I wanted to position everything a bit.

But it was also a little freeing, because at the beginning I was mostly working with how it sounded, and didn't have to worry about the meaning of it as much.

## What sounds or textures define this track?

I am a sucker for a good bass-line, and to me the rhythm of the singing, the drums, and the synth bass underneath it all is what the song was about. There were some decorations and such that I think are important, but that was the skeleton the song hung itself on.

## How this fits into the arc of the work

That's a really good question. I have been finding a lot of my recent tracks are surprising me. I'm doing some genres I never really expected to: hip hop, Latin, and pop tracks. Lickin' was a lot about making your way through the world, and this is a different theme than previous.

I don't know what that says, honestly. A lot of those older themes are certainly still present for me. But I have been trying hard to find peace within the anxiety and horror in the world, and maybe this is a sign that is peeking through!

## What were you listening to while making this?

I've been listening to a lot of Oklou, and just really paying attention to the production in it. I've also had "Run Free (Nite Version)" by Soulwax on repeat — that song just blows me away. And finally, I've been listening to "Like I Say (I Runaway)" by Nilüfer Yanya. The wall of sound when the guitars hit is just insane.
